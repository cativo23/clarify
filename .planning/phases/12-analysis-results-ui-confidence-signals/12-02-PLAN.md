<objective>
Add the navigable section index (RESULT-01): a Secciones sidebar that groups the findings by `categoria_riesgo` (D-01), shows `{categoria} ({count})` per category, and scrolls the report to the first finding of the chosen category on click (D-02). It lives inside FindingsSection, beside the anchored cards that plan 12-01 created.

Purpose: Long reports become navigable by risk area instead of a single scroll through every card, uniformly across Basic, Premium and Forensic (every tier emits `categoria_riesgo`).

Output:
- `groupFindingsByCategory` + `CategoryIndexEntry` in `composables/useFindingsReport.ts`
- `components/analysis/ReportSidebar.vue` (new)
- `components/analysis/FindingsSection.vue` (two-column layout with the index)
- Tests for the helper, the sidebar, and the section integration

Interpretation recorded here (Claude's call, overridable): the index is built from `summary.hallazgos` rather than `desglose_riesgo`. D-01 locks grouping of the findings themselves, and building from the findings guarantees every entry has a card to scroll to (D-02). `desglose_riesgo` counts only red and yellow findings (Phase 11 prompts), so a category holding only green or grey cards would be missing from the index. Each count is the number of cards the user will find in that category. Cards keep their existing severity order in the DOM; D-02 targets the first card of the category.
</objective>
